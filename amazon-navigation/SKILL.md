## Your Orders Page

**How to access:** Click "Returns & Orders" in the top right corner of any page, or go to https://www.amazon.com/gp/css/order-history

### Tab Navigation
| Tab | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Orders** | All orders (default view) |
| **Buy Again** | Previously purchased items for quick reorder |
| **Not Yet Shipped** | Orders in transit or being prepared |
| **Digital Orders** | E-books, digital subscriptions, etc. |
| **Amazon Pay** | Purchases made with Amazon Pay |
| **Cancelled Orders** | Orders that were cancelled |

### Filters
- **All orders** dropdown — Filter by order type
- **Last 3 months** dropdown — Change time range (last 30 days, 3 months, 6 months, by year)
- **Search Orders** bar — Search by item name, order number, or PO number
- **View order reports** button — Download order data for business reporting

### Per-Order Actions
Each order shows: Order placed date, Total, Ship to, Placed by, Order #

Available buttons per order:
- **Problem with order** — Report an issue
- **Track package** (yellow button) — Track shipment
- **Print packing slip** — Print for records
- **Return or replace items** — Start a return
- **Share gift receipt** — Send gift receipt
- **View order details** — Full order breakdown
- **Printable Order Summary** — Print-friendly version

## Safety Rules

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Purchase / Checkout** | NEVER click "Proceed to checkout," "Place your order," or "Buy now" without explicit user confirmation |
| **Delete / Cancel** | NEVER cancel an order or delete items without asking first |
| **Account Settings** | Read only — do not change billing, shipping, passwords, or security settings |
| **Payment info** | NEVER enter or view credit card numbers or bank details |
| **Login** | Ask the user to log in themselves — Claude cannot enter passwords |
| **Subscribe & Save** | Do not modify subscriptions without explicit permission |

## Common Workflows

### Reorder a previously purchased item
1. Click **"Returns & Orders"** in the top right
2. Click the **"Buy Again"** tab
3. Find the item → click **"Buy it again"** or **"Add to cart"**

### Search for a new product
1. Click the **search bar** at the top center
2. Type the product name → press **Enter**
3. Use left sidebar filters to narrow results
4. Click a product to view details → **"Add to cart"**

### Check order status
1. Click **"Returns & Orders"** in the top right
2. Find the order → click **"Track package"** (yellow button)

### Start a return
1. Click **"Returns & Orders"** in the top right
2. Find the order → click **"Return or replace items"**
3. Follow the on-screen prompts

### Switch between personal and business accounts
1. Hover over **"Hello, Brandon / Account"** in the top right
2. Click **"Switch Accounts"** in the right column
3. Select the account to switch to
